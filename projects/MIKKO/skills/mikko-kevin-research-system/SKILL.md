# Mikko-Centered Research System

Treat this skill as the user's personal research operating system. Mikko is the
single fixed analytical backbone and must be sufficient to carry the whole
analysis. Kevin is not a co-equal main framework: attach it only when a China or
Hong Kong question requires deeper credit, policy, flow, or pricing diagnosis.
The four-step system organizes the visible conclusion; it is not a third
analytical framework. Industry modules are also optional overlays.

## Required workflow

1. Define the cutoff time, horizon, geography, assets, and requested format.
2. Verify time-sensitive facts with current sources. Prefer official releases,
   central banks, filings, transcripts, and company investor relations.
3. Always apply the **Mikko main framework**. Read
   [references/mikko-global-layer.md](references/mikko-global-layer.md).
4. Only if the core question requires a dedicated diagnosis of China/Hong Kong
   credit, leverage, policy transmission, investor flows, RMB, property, or
   China-asset pricing—or the user explicitly asks for Kevin—attach the
   **Kevin optional overlay**. Read
   [references/kevin-china-hk-layer.md](references/kevin-china-hk-layer.md).
5. If the question concerns AI, semiconductors, storage, optical networking,
   CSP capex, or company earnings, read
   [references/ai-industry-layer.md](references/ai-industry-layer.md).
6. Organize the finished analysis into the user's fixed four-step output. Read
   [references/four-step-decision-system.md](references/four-step-decision-system.md).
7. Choose the shortest format that fully answers the request. For morning notes,
   reports, public-account articles, and one-page visuals, read
   [references/output-templates.md](references/output-templates.md).
8. Follow the user's standing language, evidence, and visualization preferences
   in [references/user-preferences.md](references/user-preferences.md).

## Framework hierarchy

Use this order and do not reverse it:

`facts -> Mikko main analysis -> optional overlays (Kevin / AI-industry) -> four-step decision output`

- Mikko is always on. It answers: What regime and shock produced the move?
  Through which rates, commodity, FX, funding, balance-sheet, policy, earnings,
  positioning, and valuation channels does it travel? What confirms or rejects
  the conclusion?
- Kevin is conditional. For China assets, it asks: Is credit actually expanding, is policy
  changing expected returns, who is buying, and has the rally moved from risk
  premium repair to earnings-backed diffusion?
- The four-step system presents the answer: What surprised, what state are we
  in, how does it map to assets, and what confirms or falsifies the conclusion?

Do not load Kevin merely because a company has Chinese revenue, a supply chain
touches China, or an A-share appears in the output. If Mikko alone explains the
question, stop at Mikko.

## Evidence rules

- Label facts, market-implied expectations, inference, and forecast separately.
- Compare actual data with consensus and prior values; direction alone is not a
  surprise.
- Treat prices as outputs first, not as explanations.
- For US rates, separate the expected policy path, inflation compensation, real
  yields, term premium, supply, positioning, and market plumbing.
- For China, never equate abundant liquidity or a rate cut with private credit
  expansion.
- For themes and companies, require a chain from catalyst to orders, revenue,
  margins, cash flow, revisions, valuation, and breadth.
- End investment conclusions with concrete watchpoints and first falsifiers.

## Default response contract

Lead with one decisive paragraph. Then provide:

1. Facts and expectations gap
2. Regime and state diagnosis
3. Transmission into relevant assets or companies
4. Scenarios, watchpoints, and falsifiers

Do not force irrelevant sections. Do not force AI into unrelated macro events.
Do not present conditional analysis as personalized investment advice.
