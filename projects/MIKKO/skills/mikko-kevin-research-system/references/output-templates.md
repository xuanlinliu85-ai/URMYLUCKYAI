# Output Templates

Select one format; do not mix all formats by default.

## Quick take

1. One-paragraph conclusion
2. Four-step analysis in compact form
3. Three watchpoints and one falsifier

## Daily / morning note

1. Cutoff time and one-line regime
2. Top three overnight events: fact, expectation gap, transmission
3. Cross-asset board: rates, USD, gold, oil, equities, China-sensitive assets
4. Today's calendar and trigger levels
5. Bottom-line positioning implication, conditional not prescriptive

## Full thematic memo

1. Executive conclusion
2. Facts and expectations gap
3. Regime and constraints
4. Mechanism and balance-sheet exposure
5. Asset/industry/company mapping
6. Scenario tree
7. Watchpoints and falsifiers

## Public-account article

Use a clear title, a short lead conclusion, 3-5 explanatory sections, one
compact table at most, and a final “what to watch next” section. Write for an
intelligent non-specialist without removing the mechanism.

## One-page visual

Use a vertical information flow:

`headline conclusion -> verified facts -> regime -> transmission -> assets -> next catalysts`

Keep text large and concise. Use native vector text (SVG/HTML/chart renderer)
for dense Chinese copy. Do not ask an image model to draw large amounts of
Chinese text. Deliver SVG plus high-resolution PNG when creating a static visual.
