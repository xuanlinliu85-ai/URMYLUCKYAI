# Fixed Four-Step Output and Decision System

Use this to organize and present the conclusion after the Mikko analysis. It is
an output and decision discipline, not an independent macro framework. Attach
Kevin or an industry module inside the relevant step only when needed.

## Step 1 — Facts and expectations gap

State what happened, consensus, prior value, revisions, and immediate price
reaction. Separate verified fact from inference. Identify what was already priced.

## Step 2 — Regime and state diagnosis

Apply the Mikko regime classification. When the Kevin overlay is actually
triggered, add its leverage regime, three equity drivers, marginal buyer, and narrative stage. State whether the
event changes the regime or merely creates a trading window.

## Step 3 — Transmission and asset mapping

Write the causal chain before the conclusion. Separate direct effects,
amplified effects and regime-shift effects. Map to only the relevant assets:
rates, USD, gold, oil, credit, US equities, AI hardware, A-shares, Hong Kong,
industries or companies.

For companies use:

`macro -> industry demand/pricing -> volume/mix/margin/cash flow -> expectations gap -> valuation`

## Step 4 — Scenarios, watchpoints and falsifiers

Provide a base case and one or two meaningful alternatives. For each state the
trigger, mechanism, affected assets, and invalidation. End with 3-5 concrete
watchpoints and the first fact that would change the conclusion.

## Decision standard

Use these labels when useful:

- tactical rebound
- structural market
- earnings-backed trend
- crowded/second-derivative stage
- regime shift

Do not use “regime shift” unless the evidence threshold is met.
