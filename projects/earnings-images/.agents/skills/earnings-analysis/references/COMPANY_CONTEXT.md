# Company Context Playbook

Do not analyze a quarter in isolation.

## Default baseline

Use:
- last 8 quarters
- last 3 fiscal years
- relevant peer snapshot
- current/historical valuation if available

## Analyze

### Business model
What actually creates revenue and profit?

### Segment economics
Which segment drives incremental growth and incremental profit?

### Competitive position
Market share, product differentiation, pricing power, cost advantage, ecosystem, switching costs.

### Industry cycle
Is the industry early/mid/late cycle? Is current margin near peak or trough?

### Capital allocation
Capex, buybacks, dividends, M&A, debt, reinvestment returns.

### Balance-sheet resilience
Liquidity, leverage, working capital, refinancing exposure.

### Thesis linkage
State explicitly:

```text
This quarter:
CONFIRMS
WEAKENS
or
CHANGES
the longer-term thesis.
```

Then explain why.
