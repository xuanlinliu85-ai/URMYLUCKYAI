# Sector Playbooks

Use only the relevant playbook. These are minimum defaults, not exhaustive checklists.

## Semiconductor / AI hardware

Core:
- revenue by segment/product
- data center / gaming / auto where applicable
- unit growth / ASP when available
- backlog / lead times / capacity
- inventory
- gross margin
- advanced packaging / memory / supply constraints
- capex
- customer concentration
- product transition risk

## SaaS / software

Core:
- ARR
- NRR
- RPO / cRPO
- billings
- customer growth
- ARPU / ACV
- gross margin
- sales efficiency
- SBC
- FCF margin
- AI monetization / cannibalization where relevant

## Banks

Core:
- NIM
- loan growth
- deposit growth
- CASA / funding mix
- NPL / special mention / delinquency
- credit cost / provision
- CET1 / capital adequacy
- fee income
- ROA / ROE
- deposit beta and liquidity

Do not use EV/EBITDA or generic FCF yield as primary valuation metrics.

## Insurance

Core:
- premium / new business
- VNB / NBV where applicable
- combined ratio / loss ratio
- embedded value
- solvency
- investment yield
- persistency
- product mix

## Consumer / retail

Core:
- volume / price / mix
- same-store sales
- channel inventory
- dealer/distributor inventory
- store count
- traffic / ticket
- gross margin
- promotion intensity
- working capital

## Internet / platform

Core:
- users / engagement
- ARPU
- ad load / monetization
- take rate
- GMV
- cloud / AI revenue
- capex
- margin
- buyback / capital return

## Resources / metals / chemicals

Core:
- realized price
- volume
- cash cost
- spread / margin
- utilization
- inventory
- capex
- reserve/resource life where relevant
- leverage
- cycle position

Use mid-cycle and replacement-cost logic where appropriate; avoid extrapolating peak margins.

## Situation overlays

### Cyclical
Ask whether current earnings are peak/trough/mid-cycle.

### Turnaround
Separate operational improvement from accounting/one-off improvement.

### High growth
Focus on durability, unit economics, reinvestment returns, and forward visibility.

### HoldCo
Consider look-through NAV and holding-company discount.

### Recent IPO
Pay extra attention to prospectus-era normalization, lockups, dilution, and short public track record.
